# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
the cp-liveset community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our standards

We expect everyone to act with empathy, respect, and good faith: giving and
gracefully accepting constructive feedback, focusing on what is best for the
community, and being patient with newcomers. Conduct that makes others feel
unwelcome or unsafe is not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — issues, pull requests,
discussions, and code review — and when an individual is representing the
project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Concerns about conduct may be reported privately to the maintainer at
<michiel.dewilde@gmail.com>. All reports will be reviewed and handled
confidentially and fairly.
